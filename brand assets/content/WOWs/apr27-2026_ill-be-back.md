## Content

In-text Youtube link: 
-
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fJSTBNTac6k

Brilliant uncertainty permeates this closing track from A Hard Day's Night. John captured the ambiguity of young love, shifting between major and minor keys to mirror romantic indecision. The song can't decide if it's hopeful or defeated, which makes it feel completely genuine.

As we mature, we learn that love doesn't always provide clear answers: that love involves soaring heights and confusing lows. When we are hurt, we can simultaneously want to stay and need to leave, feel certain and completely uncertain.

Complex emotions often require time and self-reflection. The song doesn't resolve its contradictions or choose between competing feelings. Instead, it sits with the discomfort of not knowing, acknowledging that some questions aren't ready to be answered even when the stakes are high.

Keeping your promise to return builds trust, even in the face of uncertainty. The title itself is a commitment: I'll be back. Not "I'll never leave" but rather an acknowledgment that sometimes you'll have to go. At many stages of life true love is more about the reliable return than about permanent presence.
